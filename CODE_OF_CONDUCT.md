# Code of Conduct

Reader is a small project, and we want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) to have a good experience. This code is based on the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1, whose full text applies where this summary is silent.

## Our pledge

We make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Respect different views and experiences; disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, tell the maintainer, [@OxO-106](https://github.com/OxO-106). For anything you do not want to say in public, use the repository's private report form (<https://github.com/OxO-106/epub-reader/security/advisories/new>): only the maintainer can read it, and you can say there that it is a conduct report rather than a security one.

Every report is reviewed and handled promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting is respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful. Responses follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a private warning, then a temporary ban, then a permanent ban, depending on the seriousness and whether it repeats.
