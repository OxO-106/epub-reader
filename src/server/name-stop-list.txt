# Words the name detector (translate-names.ts) never treats as names, so they are translated instead of being kept
# in English. A plain data file: add a word to a group (or start a new group) and it takes effect at the next start.
#
# Format: "[group]" starts a group, the words under it are separated by spaces or line breaks, "#" starts a comment.
# Matching ignores case. Every group is a stop-list except two with a special meaning:
#   [titles]         also dropped from names the caller supplies ("Mr. Darcy" becomes "Darcy"), and the capitalised
#                    word after a title is a name even at the start of a sentence or in a heading.
#   [abbreviations]  a full stop after one of them does not end a sentence ("Mr. Bennet"). Stop words too.
#
# Err on the side of translating: a missed name only costs a transliterated name, a masked ordinary word stays
# English in the middle of the Chinese.
